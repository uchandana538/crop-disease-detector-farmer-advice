Seasonal Planting & Crop Protection Guide

What crops to sow, disease vulnerabilities to anticipate, and optimal care season-by-season.

☀️ Summer Season (March – May)

Optimal Crops: Okra, Cucumber, Watermelon, Chili Pepper, Maize, Sunflower, Moong Bean

Key Protection & Irrigation Advice:

  • Drip / Early Irrigation: Water during pre-dawn or sunset to prevent rapid thermal evaporation and plant shock.
  • Organic Mulching: Spread paddy straw or dry crop residue (5-7 cm thick) across root zones to conserve soil moisture.
  • Sunburn & Scorch Guard: Erect 30-50% shade netting over sensitive nursery saplings during peak afternoon sun.
  • Pest Vectors: Heat encourages sucking pests like whiteflies and thrips. Spray organic neem formulation preventively.

🌧️ Monsoon Season (June – September)

Optimal Crops: Paddy (Rice), Soybean, Cotton, Groundnut, Turmeric, Ginger, Sugarcane

Key Protection & Irrigation Advice:

  • Field Drainage: Construct border trenches to evacuate standing water and prevent fungal Phytophthora root rot.
  • High Blight Risk: Warm moisture accelerates fungal spore germination. Inspect lower foliage weekly.
  • Fungicidal Shield: Spray protective contact fungicide (Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5g/L) prior to prolonged rainfall spells.
  • No Overhead Sprinklers: Avoid wetting foliage artificially; ambient precipitation provides adequate moisture.

❄️ Winter Season (October – February)

Optimal Crops: Wheat, Mustard, Chickpea, Potato, Carrot, Tomato, Cabbage, Cauliflower

Key Protection & Irrigation Advice:

  • Frost & Chill Shielding: Cover young seedling rows with light agrofiber or thatch during freezing nighttime temperatures.
  • Powdery Mildew Vigilance: Cool mornings with moderate humidity trigger powdery mildew. Spray wettable sulfur @ 3g/L.
  • Irrigation Interval: Low evapotranspiration rates mean watering intervals can be extended by 4-6 days.
  • Tillage Aeration: Deeply till and loosen heavy soils before sowing root crops to avoid tuber rot.

🌱 Universal Plant Pathology Rules (All Year Round)

  • Crop Rotation: Never plant solanaceous crops (Tomato, Potato, Eggplant) back-to-back in the same soil patch.
  • Tool Sterilization: Disinfect pruning shears in 70% isopropyl alcohol or 10% bleach solution between plants.
  • Debris Disposal: Burn or deeply bury diseased crop foliage; never mix infected leaves into compost piles.
  • Balanced Nutrition: Avoid excessive urea/nitrogen which produces soft, watery leaf tissue vulnerable to fungal penetrations.
